## PART C — Flow bugfixes
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

C1. Flow dots desync after tab switch + resize: when you leave the Flow tab
    (e.g. go to Ideas) and come back, the travelling dots / node positions are
    "off in space" for a few seconds as the window resizes. Root cause to verify:
    the Flow canvas keeps a persistent RAF loop (ensureFlowLoop) but its
    width/height + the zoom-fit (scene offset) are computed once and not recomputed
    on tab re-show or window resize, so the layout is stale until a resize event.
    Fix: when the Flow tab becomes visible again (tab switch) AND on window resize,
    re-measure the canvas clientWidth/clientHeight, re-run the zoom-fit (recompute
    the scene scale + offset so nodes stay centered and unclipped), and restart the
    dot phase so nothing looks "off". The leave-and-return must re-fit cleanly at
    the user's real ~1280×525 canvas with no jump/desync.

C2. Click the grid to deselect: on the Flow canvas, clicking empty space (the grid
    background, not a node) should DESELECT the currently-selected node. Add a
    canvas click handler: if the click is NOT on a node (hit-test miss), clear the
    selection (selectedNode = null / clear the selected styling) and hide any open
    node-detail panel. Clicking a node still selects it (existing behaviour).
