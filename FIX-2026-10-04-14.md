# Forge Console — Fix Brief 14 (2026-10-04): hub logo reads as a flat hexagon, not a cube

Web-only (web/app.js). One visual fix.

Problem: vision review of the live render confirms the hub's mark reads as a
FLAT HOLLOW HEXAGON RING (thick white hexagon, dark hollow center) — not the
3D cube logo. The 3D-ness (three shaded faces + inner Y-edges) is not visible.

Root cause (verify yourself): in `drawHubSquare` (app.js ~line 2985) the mark
scale is `s = 0.35` (hexagon spans ~±47 in logo units → ~33px tall on canvas)
while `FORGE_LOGO_STROKE_WIDTH = 25` → an ~8.75px outline stroke. That stroke is
comparable to the whole mark, so it dominates: the thin face fills (#2f2f35 /
#26262b / #1f1f23) are mostly hidden and the inner edges aren't drawn at all,
leaving a "ring" look.

Fix:
- Scale the mark up: `s ≈ 0.5` (or whatever makes the hexagon ~45-55px in the
  node's inner area, above the THE FORGE label — don't collide with the label).
- Thin the outline: use ~1.75-2px on canvas (i.e. stop multiplying the 25 by s;
  a crisp thin outline, round caps/joins) so the faces are visible inside.
- Make the cube read 3D: after filling the 3 faces, draw the three INNER cube
  edges (the "Y": from the hexagon center point to the 3 alternating
  vertices — for this logo, the lines from the face-junction point
  `FORGE_LOGO_TOP[1]` (≈ [0, -0.67]) to `FORGE_LOGO_TOP[0]`, `FORGE_LOGO_TOP[2]`,
  and to `FORGE_LOGO_TOP[3]`... precisely: the inner vertex where all 3 faces
  meet is FORGE_LOGO_TOP[1]; the three inner edges run from that vertex to the
  hexagon's top-left (-40.05,-23.66→ actually the shared corners: FORGE_LOGO_TOP[0],
  FORGE_LOGO_TOP[2], and FORGE_LOGO_TOP[3]) — i.e. connect the center junction
  to the 3 non-adjacent hexagon corners that are face boundaries). Draw them in
  the accent color (or dim white) at ~1.25-1.5px. Keep the 3 face fills
  (slightly different tones) so the shading shows.
- Keep the node glow and label placement.

Verify (live browser, real ~1280×525 canvas): the hub mark reads as a 3D cube
(hexagon silhouette + visible shaded faces + inner Y-edges), not a hollow ring;
label THE FORGE unobstructed; nothing else changed.
`node --check web/app.js` clean. No service restart (web-only).
